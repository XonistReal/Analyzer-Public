# Cosmetics: published controls and Analyzer scope

Compared October 9, 2026 against the 37 Cosmetics entries in Temple.nz's [official module catalogue](https://temple.nz/modules) and its [public settings data](https://temple.nz/assets/data/modules.json?v=be4c7e7b). The reference column summarizes setting families, not observed footage or copied descriptions. Analyzer's column describes its own 1.21.4 implementation and registered controls. Exact Analyzer defaults, bounds and setting IDs are exported in [MODULES.md](MODULES.md) and [MODULES.json](MODULES.json).

The code, procedural meshes and effects are original. Similar module or option names do not establish identical rendering, complete control coverage or compatibility with Temple's Minecraft version. This table covers the cosmetic category; [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) covers the wider catalogue, and [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) identifies the controls and events checked in game.

## Shared Analyzer controls

World-effect modules expose **Natural / Theme / Custom / Rainbow** color modes, custom RGB channels **0–255**, brightness **0.25–1.75**, color-cycle speed **0.1–4** and a brightness pulse. Hit Sounds is audio-only and has no color controls. Shape, event filters and timing remain specific to each module. These controls change procedural geometry colors; they are not a shared refractive, metallic or physically lit material system.

Cape, Pets and Crystal Chams expose Theme, Mint, Violet, Gold, Rainbow and custom RGB; Crystal Chams also has Original color. Their opacity and animation controls are independent. Item Physics, Hand Tweaks and player poses alter rendered state or transforms rather than game physics, attacks or server-visible animation.

Menu **Particles** is a separate cursor-reactive field controlled by Interface motion and particle density. **Air Effects** is a world-space field around the camera. **Sky Aurora** draws the world sky; Temple's **Aurora** entry describes a player cosmetic and is compared separately below.

## Controls and validation

Open **Workspace**, search the module name, select its card and use the scrolling inspector. **Classic** exposes the same settings by right-clicking a module; **Compact** uses its inspector. Settings persist, including numeric bounds and choices. Color channels are sliders rather than imported textures or a material editor.

Place/break visuals require actual local interactions; hurt, death, pickup, explosion, totem and lightning effects require corresponding loaded game events. Model cosmetics need the relevant rendered object: view capes, pets and player poses in third person, use first person for Hand Tweaks, and load an end crystal for Crystal Chams. The registry describes implemented source controls; the validation log states which modes received runtime checks. Neither enabling a module in a menu nor compiling its source demonstrates all of its visual modes.

Iris/Sodium compatibility and other renderer combinations have not been validated. Waving Foliage, Wet Ground, Realistic Water, Hand Chams and Glint Color remain render-pipeline work and are not presented as functional Analyzer modules.
